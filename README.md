# Nassau Candy — Product Line Profitability & Margin Performance Dashboard
Product Line Profitability & Margin Performance Dashboard for Nassau Candy Distributor — an interactive Streamlit app that transforms order-level sales and cost data into clear, actionable product- and division-level insights. Identify which SKUs truly drive profit, detect high-sales/low-margin risks, surface cost-heavy products, and prioritize repricing or supplier renegotiation using Pareto and time-series margin diagnostics.

Why this matters

Sales volume alone can be misleading. This dashboard reveals true profitability so management can make data-driven decisions on pricing, promotions, sourcing, and product rationalization.
Helps reduce margin erosion by flagging products and divisions that consume disproportionate cost or generate low margins despite strong revenue.
Key features

Robust data ingestion: upload CSV/Excel or auto-download from a public Google Drive file.
Data cleaning & validation: currency parsing, missing-value handling, and gross-profit recomputation/flagging.
Product-level metrics: sales, units, gross profit, gross margin (%), profit per unit, revenue & profit contribution.
Division-level analysis: revenue vs profit comparison, margin distribution, and contribution percentages.
Pareto concentration: identify the minimal set of products responsible for ~80% of revenue and profit.
Cost vs margin diagnostics: scatter plots, cost-heavy product flags, and risk classification (critical/high/medium).
Margin volatility: monthly margin trends and rolling volatility per product.
Factory-product mapping: visualize product-to-factory relationships on a map.
Exportable reports: download cleaned dataset, product metrics, and division metrics as CSV.
Deployment-ready: includes requirements.txt and Dockerfile for Streamlit Cloud, Render, or other Docker hosts.
